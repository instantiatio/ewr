# About Instantiatio

Instantiatio is a research programme on AI-native-first ways of working: people determine goals and value, while AI independently organizes and performs engineering work within an assignment.

The programme connects the [nine AI-native-first principles](/ewr/eng/foundations/ai-native-first/), [System Formation](/ewr/eng/foundations/system-formation/), and [Engineering Work Runtime](/ewr/eng/foundations/ewr/). It investigates how these ideas expand the systems that people and small teams can create, with attention to useful results and the total effort of obtaining them.

## iEWR in practice

iEWR is the programme's practical implementation: an application for organizing and performing engineering work with AI. [How iEWR works](/ewr/eng/how-iewr-works/) explains its structure and execution environment.

This site describes **iEWR 7.0.8 Eternal Alpha**. [Try iEWR](/ewr/eng/try/) is the practical entry; [Cases & Experiments](/ewr/eng/cases/) connects work to results and checks. Foundations provides optional full conceptual materials.
